Finishers & Playmakers: How Stars Create Goals

Raw goal and assist totals tell us how much a player produced, but underlying FBref tracking stats show how they produced it. Across the 1,518 outfield regulars who logged at least 900 minutes (90s >= 10), European football’s best attackers split into distinct tactical archetypes: high-volume target men, clinical counter-attackers, deep-lying passers, and electric ball-carrying wingers.

1. Volume Shooters vs. Clinical Finishers (10+ Goal Scorers)

Among players who scored at least 10 league goals in 2021–2022, two paths to the Golden Boot stand out. Volume strikers like Robert Lewandowski (4.80 shots/90, 35 goals) and Mohamed Salah (4.36 shots/90, 23 goals) generate relentless shot attempts, whereas clinical finishers like Son Heung-min (2.57 shots/90, converting 27% of his non-penalty shots for 23 goals) and Wissam Ben Yedder (2.31 shots/90, 26% conversion, 25 goals) score nearly as often on roughly half the shot volume.

2. Attacking Archetypes: Pure Poachers, Playmakers, and Complete Superstars

FBref’s Shot-Creating Actions (SCA) measures the two offensive actions—such as live passes, dribbles, or fouled take-ons—directly leading to a shot. Plotting Shots per 90 against SCA per 90 separates Europe’s attacking regulars into clear roles:

  • Top-Left (Pure Creators): Dimitri Payet (6.53 SCA/90), Kevin De Bruyne (5.67 SCA/90), and fullback Trent Alexander-Arnold (4.73 SCA/90) orchestrate attacks for teammates rather than shooting frequently themselves.
  • Bottom-Right (Box Poachers): Strikers like Ciro Immobile and Erling Haaland focus their touches on finishing inside the penalty area.
  • Top-Right (Dual-Threat Superstars): Kylian Mbappé (4.29 shots/90, 5.12 SCA/90), Karim Benzema, and Lionel Messi both finish and create at elite rates.

3. Ball Progression Styles: Passers vs. Ball-Carriers

Before a shot or assist even happens, teams have to move the ball toward the opponent’s goal. Comparing Progressive Passes per 90 (PasProg)—completed passes moving the ball at least 10 yards closer to goal—with Progressive Carries per 90 (CarProg)—dribbles moving the ball toward goal—reveals two completely different ways to break down defenses. Electric wingers like Vinícius Júnior (14.2 progressive carries/90) and Allan Saint-Maximin drive past defenders at their feet, deep playmakers like Toni Kroos and Trent Alexander-Arnold (8.77 progressive passes/90) slice lines with passing, and Lionel Messi sits in a corner almost by himself (10.2 progressive passes/90 and 12.1 progressive carries/90).
